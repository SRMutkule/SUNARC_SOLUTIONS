1. Introduction

SUNARC SOLUTIONS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit our website or contact us.

2. Information We May Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry, you may provide:

  • Your name

  • Phone number

  • Other information you choose to include in your message

3. How We Use Information

Information shared with us may be used to:

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared where necessary to provide requested services, comply with applicable legal obligations or protect legitimate business interests.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information. However, this may limit our ability to respond to your enquiry. You can contact us to ask about information you have shared.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
