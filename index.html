Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions about solar energy, installations, net metering, and our services.

Suitability depends on roof or site conditions, sunlight availability, electricity consumption, and technical requirements. A professional site evaluation can help identify suitable solar options.

Where permitted, net metering accounts for electricity imported from and exported to the grid under applicable utility regulations. Eligibility, billing arrangements, and approval procedures may vary.
